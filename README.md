# US-Total-Vehicle-Sales

- The observation_date variable identifies the month associated with each observation. The TOTALSA variable represents the total number of vehicles sold in the United States, expressed in millions of units at a seasonally adjusted annual rate.
- The data are produced by the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) and are distributed through the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis's FRED database. FRED identifies the series as part of the BEA's Supplemental Estimates, Motor Vehicles release. The data are collected and reported on a monthly basis. The BEA provides estimates of motor vehicle sales and inventories, including total vehicle sales. The series is seasonally adjusted, meaning seasonal patterns in vehicle sales are accounted for in the published measure. 
- I chose this data set because I am interested in seeing how changes in vehicle demand affect how many vehicles manufacturers produce and how dealerships manage their inventory.
